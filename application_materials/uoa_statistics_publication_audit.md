# Publication audit for the University of Auckland Statistics application

## Result

- The original CV and website bibliography files each contained 28 records.
- The ORCID-linked OpenAlex author record identified 21 additional published
  journal articles absent from both local bibliography files.
- The repaired bibliography now contains 49 source records:
  - 48 papers or preprints displayed in the application CV;
  - one Zenodo software-release record retained in the source bibliography but
    excluded from the publication list.

The application CV separates:

1. short-author publications and preprints; and
2. large-collaboration publications for which Avi Vajpeyi is a listed
   collaboration author.

The two 2026 arXiv manuscripts remain visibly labelled as preprints. The
large-collaboration section includes the previously omitted 2025 COMPAS methods
paper and 20 additional LVK papers.

## Sources and method

- ORCID: <https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4146-1132>
- OpenAlex author record: <https://openalex.org/A5006869251>
- COMPAS reference record:
  <https://compas.readthedocs.io/en/latest/pages/references.html>

OpenAlex results were matched to the local BibTeX records using normalised DOI
values. Software releases, datasets, duplicate preprint/journal records, and
arXiv DOI duplicates were not promoted to journal articles.

## Remaining caution

OpenAlex and ORCID are useful discovery sources but are not authoritative
authorship statements. Before submission, spot-check the collaboration
authorship policy and the author list for any paper whose inclusion is
unexpected. In particular, verify that all 20 recovered LVK papers correspond
to the period in which collaboration authorship applied to Avi Vajpeyi.
