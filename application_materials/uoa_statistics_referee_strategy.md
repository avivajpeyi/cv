# Referee strategy

## Recommended three

1. **Renate Meyer -- current research and departmental fit**

   Renate can provide the most direct evidence that the Statistics appointment
   is a continuation of an established methodological programme rather than an
   unsupported disciplinary pivot. Ask her to address current research,
   independence, collaboration, mentoring, funding potential, and contribution
   to the Department.

2. **Chunyang Chen -- teaching**

   Teaching is the least self-evident part of the written record. Chunyang can
   independently verify tutorial delivery, assessment authorship, automated
   grading, marking, sustained FIT3170 mentoring, client engagement, and
   contribution to project-based course delivery. Ask for both a brief teaching
   confirmation for the application and permission to list him as a referee.

3. **Eric Thrane -- independent research development**

   As PhD supervisor, Eric can speak to the origin of the applicant's research
   independence, quantitative training, publication leadership, collaboration,
   and academic trajectory. His perspective is external to the current
   Auckland network and complements Renate and Chunyang.

## Strong alternatives

- **Patricio Maturana-Russel:** strongest alternative when the panel would
  benefit more from a recent external statistical-methodology collaborator than
  from the PhD-supervisor perspective. Particularly useful for P-splines,
  evidence estimation, the AUT presentation, and cross-institutional work.
- **Matt Edwards:** valuable for current departmental collaboration, service,
  and your contribution as a thesis examiner. His evidence overlaps more with Renate's and gives
  the panel two internal Auckland voices, so he is better as a fourth referee or
  replacement if another person is unavailable.

## Recommended order of contact

Ask Renate, Chunyang, and Eric first. If Eric cannot respond on the deadline or
cannot comment on the recent statistical trajectory, ask Patricio. Include Matt
as a fourth referee if the application permits four names. Contact all three
recommended referees immediately rather than waiting for the final CV; send the
position description now and follow with the tailored documents.
