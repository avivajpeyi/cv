# University of Auckland Statistics CV: content blueprint

This is a content plan for a position-specific CV. It should become a separate
CV variant, rather than overwriting the general academic CV.

## 1. Header and research profile

Place this immediately below contact details:

> Bayesian statistician and computational researcher developing non-parametric,
> time-series, and scalable inference methods for complex scientific data. My
> work combines Bayesian spectral modelling, evidence estimation, variational
> inference, and machine learning, with applications in gravitational-wave
> astronomy and space-based interferometry.

## 3. Research programme and selected contributions

Use a short section before the complete publication list. Suggested introduction:

> My research develops Bayesian and computational methods for inference from
> noisy, correlated, high-dimensional data. A current focus is flexible
> spectral-density modelling and reliable uncertainty quantification for
> gravitational-wave detectors. I combine methodological development with
> reproducible scientific software and collaborative, application-led research.

Then feature four contributions, each in 2--3 lines:

1. **Bayesian spectral modelling:** P-spline estimation of LISA noise and
   multivariate spectral-density matrices. Demonstrated the method on EEG,
   financial, and astrophysical time series at the 2025 AUT Mathematical
   Modelling and Analytics Symposium.
2. **Bayesian computation:** evidence estimation using density approximation;
   variational inference for correlated detector noise.
3. **Scalable statistical software:** development of `parallel_bilby` and
   contributions to `asimov`.
4. **Machine learning with statistical safeguards:** neural surrogates and
   active learning in gravitational-wave inference; clearly distinguish this
   from LLM-specific expertise.

## 4. Refereed publications

Use four subsections, preserving the bibliographic order and authorship exactly:

1. Refereed journal articles
2. Refereed conference proceedings, if any
3. Preprints / submitted manuscripts
4. Large-collaboration publications

Mark your name in bold. Do not call arXiv manuscripts "refereed". The existing
general CV's `nocite{*}` bibliography should be audited because a complete
publication record is required and the selection panel needs categories that
are immediately intelligible.
