## Draft reference letter

**Dear members of the Appointment Committee,**

I am pleased to provide a teaching reference for Dr Avi Vajpeyi in support of
his application for the Lecturer position in the Department of Statistics at
the University of Auckland. I am Professor and Chair of Software Engineering
and AI at the Technical University of Munich and was previously a member of
the Faculty of Information Technology at Monash University. I worked with Avi
when he served as a teaching assistant in FIT9136 Algorithms and Programming
Foundations in Python in 2020 and FIT3170 Software Engineering Practice in 2021
and 2022.

FIT9136 was delivered during the rapid transition to online teaching at the
start of the COVID-19 pandemic. Avi adapted well to this difficult environment.
He supported students through online laboratory sessions and live Zoom
tutorials, where he explained programming and algorithmic concepts and
demonstrated solutions. He also contributed to the development of assignments
and examinations with the teaching team, graded student work, and took the
initiative to help develop automated grading tools. These tools reduced the
manual workload on the teaching team while helping us provide consistent and
timely assessment.

In FIT3170, Avi supported a project-based software-engineering course in which
students worked in large agile teams with external clients. Across 2021 and
2022, he mentored the Automated Grading, OwlEyes, Machine-Learning Video
Editing, and Astrophysics Visualisation teams. His responsibilities went well
beyond answering technical questions. He monitored the teams' use of the
Scaled Agile Framework, including their documentation, meetings, and division
of responsibilities. He advised students on suitable technical approaches,
helped them address disagreements and team-management problems, assessed their
work, and communicated expectations clearly. He also helped identify external
clients and guest lecturers, which strengthened the authentic, industry-facing
character of the course.

Avi's official 2021 FIT3170 teaching report provides quantitative evidence of
the quality of his contribution. Seventeen students completed the evaluation.
His raw student-satisfaction score was 88/100 and his weighted score was
98/100, both in Monash's "Very High" band, which is classified as exceeding
expectations. The corresponding faculty average was 82/100. Sixteen of the
seventeen respondents rated his teaching either High or Very High. Although
the response count should be kept in mind, these results are consistent with
my experience of Avi as an approachable, conscientious, and effective teaching
assistant.

Avi's formal university teaching experience at that stage was as a teaching
assistant rather than as an independent course coordinator. Within that role,
however, he demonstrated initiative, reliability, clear communication, and a
strong commitment to student learning. He was able to explain technical
material, mentor students through open-ended projects, handle interpersonal
issues constructively, and contribute to assessment and course delivery as a
collegial member of the teaching team. I believe these experiences provide a
strong foundation for his development as a lecturer, particularly in applied,
computational, and project-based Statistics and Data Science teaching.

I am happy to recommend Avi for the Lecturer position. Please feel free to
contact me at chun-yang.chen@tum.de if I can provide any further information.

**Yours sincerely,**

Professor Chunyang Chen  
Chair of Software Engineering and AI  
Technical University of Munich  
chun-yang.chen@tum.de
